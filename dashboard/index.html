Before you farm anything

  • Implied value is a claim, not a price. It says what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued, or what it would trade at.
  • No token recorded is not proof there is none. The snapshot is DefiLlama's record on one day; a protocol may have a token the record misses, or issue one tomorrow.
  • Never pay to claim. Real airdrops are claimed from the protocol's own page, for gas only. Anyone asking for a fee, a seed phrase or a wallet connection to "unlock" a claim is running a scam.
  • Farm with money you can lose. A position can fall, a bridge can fail, a contract can be exploited. The farm plans size positions small on purpose.
